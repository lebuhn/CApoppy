# CApoppy

California poppy (*Eschscholzia californica*) petal work that isn't part of the
Dangermond Preserve study: the 2022 SFSU campus petal weight, water and CR6
temperature data, the differential water loss / nyctinasty paper, the 2024 student
petal datasets, and the Schoen & Ashman floral longevity model.

The 2025 Dangermond Preserve microclimate and petal ecophysiology study (loggers,
porometer, petal scans and segmentation, decision log) moved to
[poppy-microclimate](https://github.com/lebuhn/poppy-microclimate) on 2026-10-09.
